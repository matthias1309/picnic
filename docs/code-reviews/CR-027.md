## Summary

Extends `CATEGORY_RULES` so the products found misfiled or uncategorised in
the live DB review land in the right category in future: jarred tomatoes,
olives and pickles → `canned`; tea, "Mandeln gehackt", plum tomatoes,
"Butter-Chicken" and "Chocolate Chunks" no longer fall to a false-friend
keyword; Tortelloni and Cup Noodles → `ready_meals`; seasonings → `condiments`;
dishwasher salt, vinegar spray, drain cleaner and "E-Block" → `household`;
"Taschentücher" → `personal_care`; pet food → `other`; Porree → `vegetables`.

Files:
- `backend/services/category_service.py` — rules only; `categorize()` and
  `apply_rules()` are unchanged
- `backend/tests/test_categories.py` — TC-027-01..05
- `docs/requirements/REQ-027.md`, `docs/architecture/ARCH-027.md`,
  `docs/test-specs/TEST-027.md`

No schema change, no migration, no new dependency, no live-DB or production
change.
