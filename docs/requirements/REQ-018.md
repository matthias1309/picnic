# REQ-018 — Receipt List and Detail Show the Effective (Delivery) Date

**Status:** approved
**Created:** 2026-08-16
**Traced by:** ARCH-018, TEST-018

## User Story

As the **account owner**,
I want **the receipts list and receipt detail page to show the date I actually
received my groceries**,
in order to **find a specific receipt by date without being misled by an
unrelated internal timestamp**.

## Context

Reported while investigating why the price history for "Paprika rot" showed
0€ points on 15.4. and 22.4.2026 with, from the user's point of view, "no
receipts that old" visible in the Receipts list.

Investigation (read-only against production `picnic.db`) found:

- The 0€ price points are correct — both dates are genuine "Gratis"
  promotions on the Picnic invoice (confirmed against the raw source email:
  original price struck through, final price 0.00€, "Gratis" badge). Not a
  bug, out of scope here.
- The receipts for those dates (`receipts.id` 17 and 19) exist and are
  correctly ordered — `receipt_service.list_receipts` sorts by
  `Receipt.effective_date` (`coalesce(delivery_date, received_date)`), so
  April receipts are exactly where they belong, a page further back.
- **The actual bug:** `GET /receipts` and `GET /receipts/{id}` only expose
  `received_date`, and the frontend (`ReceiptList.tsx`, `ReceiptDetail.tsx`)
  displays that field. For every receipt reprocessed in the 2026-06-16/17
  bulk re-parse (see `learnings.md`, "Restart Gunicorn after every Uberspace
  deploy"), `received_date` is the *reprocessing* timestamp
  (2026-06-16/17, in the early morning), not when the email actually arrived
  or the groceries were delivered. `delivery_date` (parsed from the invoice
  HTML) correctly holds the real date and already drives sort order and the
  price-history chart — it just isn't shown.

Net effect: every receipt from 2026-04-08 through 2026-06-15 displays a
label of "16.6.2026" or "17.6.2026" in the UI, regardless of when it was
actually delivered. A user scanning the list sees a wall of near-identical
mid-June labels and reasonably concludes that's where the history ends, even
though the (correctly sorted) rows underneath are genuinely from April and
May.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-018-01: Receipt list shows the effective date

```gherkin
Given a receipt whose delivery_date (2026-04-15) differs from its
  received_date (2026-06-17, from a later reprocessing run)
When the user opens the receipts list
Then the entry for that receipt displays 15.4.2026, not 17.6.2026
```

### AC-018-02: Receipt detail shows the effective date

```gherkin
Given the same receipt as AC-018-01
When the user opens that receipt's detail page
Then the heading displays 15.4.2026, not 17.6.2026
```

### AC-018-03: Falls back to received_date when no delivery_date was parsed

```gherkin
Given a receipt with no delivery_date (the invoice HTML had no parseable
  delivery sentence, e.g. receipt id 1 in production)
When the user views that receipt in the list or its detail page
Then the displayed date is its received_date (unchanged behavior)
```

### AC-018-04: No regression on existing receipt list/detail behavior

```gherkin
Given the existing receipts API and UI test suite (TC-003-01, TC-003-02,
  TC-005-04, TC-009-05 through TC-009-07, TC-013-05)
When the change is applied
Then sort order, pagination, from_address, item counts, totals, order-number
  grouping, and delete behavior are all unchanged
```

## Notes

**Out of Scope:**
- The 0€ "Gratis" price points themselves — confirmed correct against the
  source email, not a defect.
- Changing how `received_date` or `delivery_date` are stored or parsed.
- Backfilling/correcting `received_date` for the already-reprocessed
  receipts — the fix is to display the field that is already correct
  (`effective_date`), not to rewrite historical data.
