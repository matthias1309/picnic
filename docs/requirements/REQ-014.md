# REQ-014 — Parse the Delivery Date from the Invoice HTML

**Status:** draft
**Created:** 2026-06-16
**Traced by:** ARCH-014, TEST-014

## User Story

As the **account owner**,
I want **each receipt to be dated by the delivery date stated in the invoice
email rather than by the email's delivery timestamp**,
in order to **place receipts correctly in time even when an old invoice is
re-sent or re-delivered, so my charts and statistics stay accurate**.

## Context

Today a receipt's date comes exclusively from the email's `Date` header
(`backend/main.py:80-88`), which is stored as `Receipt.received_date`
(`backend/models.py`) and copied into every `PriceHistory.recorded_date`
(`backend/services/receipt_service.py`). All time-based queries — spending
trends, monthly budget, date filters — use `Receipt.received_date`
(`backend/services/stats_service.py`, `backend/services/receipt_service.py`).

The HTML parser (`ReceiptParser`, `backend/imap/parser.py`) extracts items and
the stated total but **no date** — `ParsedReceipt` has only `items` and
`stated_total_cents` (`parser.py:31-36`).

**Consequence:** if Picnic re-sends an older invoice (e.g. a May delivery
re-delivered on 16 June), the email `Date` header is the resend date, so the
receipt is filed under the wrong day/week/month and skews all statistics. The
correct date is present in the email body, in a sentence such as:

```
hier ist der Bon zu deiner Lieferung von Montag 15 Juni 2026
```

This requirement extracts that delivery date from the HTML and uses it as the
canonical date for the receipt, falling back to the email `Date` header only
when the body has no parseable delivery date (older formats, unexpected
wording).

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-014-01: Parse the delivery date from the invoice body

```gherkin
Given an invoice whose body contains
  "hier ist der Bon zu deiner Lieferung von Montag 15 Juni 2026"
When ReceiptParser.parse() is called
Then the parsed receipt carries delivery_date = 2026-06-15
```

### AC-014-02: German month names are recognised

```gherkin
Given delivery sentences using the German month names
  (Januar, Februar, März, April, Mai, Juni, Juli, August,
   September, Oktober, November, Dezember)
When ReceiptParser.parse() is called for each
Then the corresponding calendar month is parsed correctly
```

### AC-014-03: Missing or unparseable delivery date yields None

```gherkin
Given an invoice whose body has no recognisable
  "Lieferung von <weekday> <day> <month> <year>" sentence
When ReceiptParser.parse() is called
Then delivery_date is None
And parsing still succeeds (items and total are extracted as before)
```

### AC-014-04: The delivery date dates the stored receipt

```gherkin
Given a parsed receipt with delivery_date = 2026-06-15
  re-delivered by email on 2026-06-16
When the receipt is stored
Then Receipt.delivery_date is 2026-06-15
And every PriceHistory.recorded_date for that receipt is 2026-06-15
```

### AC-014-05: Fallback to the email date when no delivery date is parsed

```gherkin
Given a parsed receipt whose delivery_date is None
When the receipt is stored
Then the receipt is dated by the email's Date header (received_date),
  preserving today's behaviour for older invoice formats
```

### AC-014-06: Statistics and charts use the delivery date

```gherkin
Given a May delivery re-delivered by email in June
When spending trends, monthly budget, and date-filtered receipts are computed
Then the receipt is counted in May (its delivery date), not June
```

## Notes

**Implementation approach:**
- `ReceiptParser` extracts the delivery date from the body text with a tolerant
  regex, e.g. `Lieferung von\s+\w+\s+(\d{1,2})\s+(<month>)\s+(\d{4})`, mapping
  German month names (case-insensitive, incl. "März") to month numbers. The
  weekday ("Montag") is ignored for parsing and only anchors the match.
- `ParsedReceipt` gains a `delivery_date: date | None` field.
- `Receipt` gains a nullable `delivery_date` column; `received_date` is kept as
  email metadata (audit trail / dedup fallback). **DB schema change — reviewed
  by a human before the production DB is migrated (CLAUDE.md).**
- The receipt's *effective date* used everywhere (PriceHistory.recorded_date,
  stats, filters) is `delivery_date` when present, else `received_date`. Prefer
  a single derived accessor over duplicating the coalesce logic
  (DRY; `stats_service.py` and `receipt_service.py` both consume it).
- Decision to confirm in ARCH-014: introduce a dedicated `delivery_date` column
  (chosen here, non-destructive, keeps the email timestamp) vs. overwriting
  `received_date` in place.

**Out of Scope:**
- Re-parsing / re-dating already-imported receipts — manual re-parse step, same
  as REQ-008/REQ-012/REQ-013 (reset `processed = False` and re-run).
- Time-of-day on the delivery date (date granularity is sufficient for trends).
- Robust deduplication of forwarded emails with a rewritten Message-ID
  (separate concern; track independently if needed).
- Locales other than German and wordings other than "Lieferung von …".

**Testing Strategy:**
- Parser unit tests: the example sentence → 2026-06-15; each German month name;
  "März" with umlaut; a body without the sentence → None; no regression on the
  existing fixtures (`picnic_receipt.html`, `picnic_receipt_current.html`,
  `picnic_receipt_forwarded.html`).
- Service test: a parsed receipt with a delivery date earlier than its email
  date is stored and dated by the delivery date (AC-014-04), and the None case
  falls back to the email date (AC-014-05).
- Stats test: the re-delivery scenario lands in the delivery month (AC-014-06).
- See TEST-014.
